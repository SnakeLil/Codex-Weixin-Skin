Codex WeChat Skin Studio
========================

1. Save your work and quit Codex yourself.
2. Double-click “Install Codex WeChat Skin.app”.
3. Click “安装并启动微信主题”.

The installer never force-quits Codex and does not modify the official app bundle.
Community DMGs are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks the first
launch, right-click the app and choose Open, or use Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
Source code: https://github.com/SnakeLil/Codex-Weixin-Skin
